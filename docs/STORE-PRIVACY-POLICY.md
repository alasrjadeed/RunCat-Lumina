# Privacy Policy — RunCat Lumina

_Last updated: 2026-10-16_

Publish this page at `https://alasarjadeed.com/privacy` and use that URL in the
Microsoft Store listing.

---

**RunCat Lumina** ("the app") is developed by AL ASAR JADEED.

**The app does not collect any data.**

- No personal data is collected, transmitted, or sold.
- The app makes **no network connections** and works fully offline.
- No analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, no trackers, no cookies.
- No account or sign-in is required.
- The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files
  beyond its own settings file.

**Local storage.** The app stores your preferences (icon color, dark/light mode,
speed setting) in a local settings file on your device (`config.json` under your
user profile). This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you
uninstall the app (you may delete the file manually afterwards).

**External links.** The app can open your default web browser to the author's
website and to the credits page of an open-source sprite project. Opening a link
is entirely optional and performed by your browser; the app itself does not
transmit any information when you do so.

**Children.** The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone,
including children.

**Changes.** If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published
on this page.
